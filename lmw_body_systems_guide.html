How it works Calbom

Liver detox is described in two enzymatic phases: Phase I (cytochrome P450 enzymes convert fat-soluble toxins to water-soluble intermediates) and Phase II (conjugation - intermediates are bound to carrier molecules for excretion). Imbalance between the two phases, especially fast Phase I with slow Phase II, is described as allowing reactive 'sticky' intermediates to accumulate, which is linked in cited research to increased cancer susceptibility (referencing a Turin, Italy chemical-plant worker bladder cancer study).

⚠ Avoid: Alcohol, drugs, caffeine, nicotine, fried foods, meats, cheese, margarine, hydrogenated oils, aspartame; grapefruit specifically avoided during active cleansing (naringin inhibits CYP3A enzymes)
